Milestone II – Progress Report

1. Progress Since Milestone I

Milestone I defined the idea (AI- and IoT-based wind energy harvesting from vehicle-induced airflow). Since then we have turned it into a working software prototype: it simulates a roadside turbine, calculates power, detects faults and shows everything on a live dashboard. Real hardware is the next stage.
Planned in Milestone IAchieved by Milestone II
Predict airflow and power from trafficWorking model: traffic speed and density → airflow → expected power

How it works (simple formulas):
Airflow = speed × traffic ÷ 1000
Expected power = 0.5 × airflow × airflow
Actual power = expected power ± small random change (noise)
Alert if vibration is above 0.5 or actual power is less than half of expected power.

Note: these formulas are simplified placeholders and the numbers are simulated, not measured from a real turbine. They will be replaced with real sensor data and calibrated values. The prototype code was written with AI assistance (Claude).

7. Next Steps

  1. Build a small model turbine and test it with a fan or passing-air source.
  2. Read real sensor data (RPM, vibration, voltage, current) with a microcontroller.
  3. Log the data and use it to train a simple ML model for power prediction and fault detection.
  4. Replace the placeholder formulas with calibrated ones.
